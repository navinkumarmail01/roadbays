Terms & Conditions (Program Summary) tap to open

Service nature. Roadbays provides referral and/or arrangement-based roadside assistance, subject to feasibility, accessibility, traffic, weather and other factors beyond the company’s control.

Chargeable items. Consumables, parts, penalties/fines, tolls, fuel costs, special salvage/extraction charges and any other third-party or chargeable expenses are payable by the customer.

External factors & delays. Response time may be impacted by traffic, location accessibility and other external conditions. If the vehicle is in a ditch/valley/inaccessible area, special salvage equipment may be arranged on chargeable basis; the program is not responsible for consequential damage during such extraction.

Adverse weather. In floods/heavy rain/thunder etc., primary focus is passenger safety; vehicle recovery may be delayed until conditions permit.

Right to refusal. Roadbays may refuse benefits (without refund) if false/incorrect information is provided or eligibility conditions are not met. Membership is not valid for commercial/rental/business/industrial/educational use. Membership is non-transferable and valid in India (except islands).

Liability. Roadbays is not liable for indirect losses, loss of data/income, or consequential loss due to defects or repair/replacement processes.

Cancellation. “No questions asked” cancellation may be available within 7 days of purchase only if no service/benefit has been availed up to the cancellation request date.

Accidental cases. Where police/FIR/court possession exists, assistance can be activated only after clearance/release and proper handover.

Disputes. Courts within New Delhi jurisdiction shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

Non-Covered Events (Examples) tap to open

Roadside Assistance does not cover certain non-safety or convenience issues and routine maintenance pickup/drop. Examples include:

  • Faulty fuel gauge, speedometer not working
  • Air-conditioning not working
  • Damaged mirrors where vision is not obstructed
  • Sunroof/windows issues when weather is fair and there is no security risk
  • ABS/airbag/traction-control or other non-safety warning lights illuminated
  • Seat adjustor fault where vehicle can be safely driven
  • Wiper fault when weather is fair
  • Vehicle pick-up and drop for routine maintenance is not included
Special Exclusions tap to open
  • Motor racing, rallies, speed/duration tests, practice runs, or operation outside official roads
  • War/riots/uprising/terrorism, mass political events; nuclear/radiation events; earthquakes/freak weather phenomena

  • Immobilization caused by police/authority intervention
  • Use against owner manual recommendations; non-roadworthy condition; improper servicing
  • Consequential costs/damage to property due to breakdown

Communications. You may receive SMS/email communications regarding services/product features. You may request opt-out where applicable.
